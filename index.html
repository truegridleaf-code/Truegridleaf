FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a business website cost?+
Our business websites start from ₹4,999. The final cost depends on the number of pages, features, and customisation required. Contact us for a personalised quote.
Do you provide WordPress websites?+
Yes, we specialise in WordPress website design and development, including custom themes and WooCommerce stores.
Can you redesign my existing website?+
Absolutely. We offer complete website redesign services to refresh your online presence with modern design and improved functionality.
Do you help with Google Business Profile?+
Yes, we provide Google Business Profile setup and optimisation, including images, posts, review response systems and maps visibility.
Do you provide Local SEO services?+
Yes, our Local SEO services include keyword research, on-page optimisation, local content, GBP optimisation and SEO audits to improve local visibility.
Can you manage website maintenance?+
Yes, we offer ongoing website maintenance plans including updates, backups, speed optimisation and security monitoring.
Do you offer domain and hosting setup?+
Yes, we help with domain registration and hosting setup as part of our website packages.
Can you create eCommerce websites?+
Yes, we build WooCommerce and Shopify stores with product pages, payment integration and mobile-friendly design.
Do you provide AI automation?+
Yes, we offer AI chatbot integration, appointment automation, lead qualification, WhatsApp workflows and FAQ automation.
How long does a website take?+
A simple business website typically takes 5–10 working days. More complex projects like eCommerce stores may take 2–4 weeks depending on requirements.
